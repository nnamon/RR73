# Getting started

Install RR73, set your callsign and grid, and start audio. RR73 runs in Safari on an iPhone or iPad, in Chrome or Edge on a computer, and in the desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux. [Your radio](your-radio.md) covers the cable, rig control and the desktop app.

## On an iPhone

1. Open RR73 in Safari.
2. Choose Share › Add to Home Screen.
3. Open RR73 from its Home Screen icon.

RR73 opened from the Home Screen starts and decodes with no network connection.

## On a computer

Open RR73 in Chrome or Edge. Rig control works only in these browsers and in the desktop app.

## Callsign and grid

1. Tap Settings, the gear at the right end of Operate's bar.
2. Under Station, enter your Callsign.
3. Enter your Grid, 4 or 6 characters, or tap Use GPS.

RR73 transmits only with both set, and until then the status line reads `Set callsign and grid in Settings`. FT8 and FT4 messages carry the first 4 characters of the grid. Name and QTH are optional and used only by RTTY macros.

## Starting audio

1. Connect the radio's audio. [Your radio](your-radio.md) has the cables and levels.
2. Tap Start audio on Operate.
3. Allow the microphone if the browser asks.

The start chime keys the radio through VOX. Turn off Start chime in Settings › Radio and audio if you do not want that.

Decodes appear 13.5 s into each 15 s FT8 slot and 6 s into each 7.5 s FT4 slot.

Tx goes off when audio stops, and the strip shows the time and the reason. Tap Resume audio to carry on. iOS stops the microphone while RR73 is in the background, so on an iPhone audio stops when you leave the app.

## The bar along the top

- Tap the band, such as 20m FT8 14.074, to open Change band. Without rig control, set the radio to this dial.
- The Clock badge shows while the clock is 1 s or more off. Tap it to open Timing. [Timing and the clock](timing.md) explains it.
- The radio's badge shows the rig control connection: green when connected, amber while connecting or when the band, mode or filter needs changing, red with no connection. Tap it to open Rig control.
- Spots shows while Activations, POTA spots or Cluster spots is on in Settings › Optional features, or Show who heard you in Settings › PSK Reporter.

On an iPad or a computer, the bar stays above every screen, with tabs for Operate, Log, Spots and Settings. Other screens show On air or Tx on, with Halt, while Tx is on.
