# Practice band

Turn on Practice band in Settings › Practice band to operate a simulated band with no radio. It has FT8 and FT4 stations with CQs, callers and a DXpedition, and RTTY stations in RTTY.

## Starting

1. Turn on Practice band. The band starts at once and Operate shows a Practice badge.
2. Call CQ, call a station or start Hunt as on the air.

Crowded band adds more stations. It is off by default.

## Tx audio and the radio

Play Tx audio, on by default, plays practice transmissions and keys a connected radio, by VOX or by CAT. Turn it off to practise with the radio silent and unkeyed.

## The practice log

Practice QSOs go to the Practice log, kept apart from your log. Log opens it while the practice band is on. Clear practice log, in Settings › Practice band, empties it, with Undo.

The practice band also pauses reading Wavelog's log, PSK Reporter reports and Web SDR. Back up the log and Import a log wait until it is off.

## Scenarios

Practice scenario, in ••• on Operate, sets how the stations that answer your CQ, or that you call, behave. The rest of the band carries on as before. The status line names a new scenario, such as `Practice · Pile-up`.

- Random band, the default: each station behaves as on a real band.
- One caller: one station answers a CQ and completes the QSO.
- Pile-up: 3 to 5 stations answer a CQ at once and keep calling.
- Caller goes quiet: the station that answers a CQ stops after the report.
- Caller misses RR73: the caller misses the first RR73 and sends the R-report again.
- Activation callers: a caller answers each CQ, and another tail-ends most QSOs.
- Weak and crowded: 1 to 3 callers near −22 dB on a crowded band in raised noise.
- Station answers: a station calling CQ answers you.
- Station works another: a station calling CQ works another station, ends with RR73 or 73, then answers the next call.
- Station answers someone else: a station you call answers another caller each time.
- Station never answers: a station calling CQ never answers you.
- CQ DX and CQ NA: stations call CQ DX and CQ NA and answer only the stations they asked for.
- Hunt: busy band. Many stations call CQ, and those weaker than −10 dB do not hear you.
- Hunt: a station calls you. The station you call stays silent and another station calls you.
- Contest exchanges: CQ FD, CQ RU and CQ TEST on the band, and callers in the Field Day, Roundup and European VHF formats.
- Worked stations: the practice log gets QSOs from today and earlier days with stations calling CQ.
- New ones: CQs from DXCC entities, zones and grids not in the practice log.
- Clock off: every station is 1.5 s early or late.
- Fox (DXpedition): a Fox works 3 Hounds a slot and answers you after a few calls at 1000 Hz or above. Turn on Hound in ••• to work it.
