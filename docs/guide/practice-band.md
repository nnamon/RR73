# Practice band

The practice band simulates a band of FT8 and FT4 stations, with CQs, callers and a DXpedition, and RTTY stations in RTTY. It needs no radio. Use it to learn Operate before going on the air.

## Starting

1. Open Settings › Practice band and turn on Practice band. The band starts at once.
2. Return to Operate. A Practice badge shows beside the band.
3. Call CQ, call a station or start Hunt as on the air.

Crowded band fills the band with more stations. It is off by default.

## Tx audio and the radio

Play Tx audio is on by default. Practice transmissions then play their audio and key a connected radio: by VOX, or by CAT where rig control keys the radio. Turn Play Tx audio off to practise with the radio silent. Off, practice plays no Tx audio and keys nothing.

## The practice log

Practice QSOs go to the Practice log, apart from your log. While the practice band is on, Log opens the Practice log. Clear practice log, in Settings › Practice band, empties it, with Undo.

While the practice band is on, Wavelog's log is not read, PSK Reporter reports pause, and Web SDR pauses. Back up the log and Import a log wait until it is off.

## Scenarios

With the practice band on, ••• on Operate lists the scenarios, such as Practice: Pile-up. The one chosen sets how the stations that answer your CQ, or that you call, behave. The rest of the band carries on as before. The status line names the scenario on a change, such as `Practice · Pile-up`.

- Random band: each station behaves as on a real band. This is the default.
- One caller: one station answers a CQ and completes the QSO.
- Pile-up: 3 to 5 stations answer a CQ at once and keep calling.
- Caller goes quiet: the station that answers a CQ stops after the report.
- Caller misses RR73: the caller misses the first RR73 and sends the R-report again.
- Activation callers: a caller answers each CQ, and another tail-ends most QSOs.
- Weak and crowded: 1 to 3 callers near −22 dB on a crowded band in raised noise.
- Station answers: a station calling CQ answers you.
- Station works another: a station calling CQ works another station, ends with RR73 or 73, then answers the next call.
- Station answers someone else: a station you call answers another caller each time you call.
- Station never answers: a station calling CQ never answers you.
- CQ DX and CQ NA: stations call CQ DX and CQ NA and answer only the stations they asked for.
- Hunt: busy band. Many stations call CQ. Those weaker than −10 dB do not hear you.
- Hunt: a station calls you. The station you call stays silent, and another station calls you.
- Contest exchanges: CQ FD, CQ RU and CQ TEST on the band, and callers to your CQ in the Field Day, Roundup and European VHF formats.
- Worked stations: the practice log gets QSOs from today and earlier days with stations calling CQ on the band.
- New ones: CQs from DXCC entities, zones and grids the practice log does not hold.
- Clock off: every station is 1.5 s early or late.
- Fox (DXpedition): a Fox works 3 Hounds a slot and answers you after a few calls at 1000 Hz or above. Turn on Hound in ••• to work it.
