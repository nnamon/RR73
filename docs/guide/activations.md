# Activations

Turn on Activations in Settings › Optional features, then start one from Spots. An activation ties the QSOs you log to one or more POTA, SOTA, WWFF or IOTA references.

## Starting an activation

1. Open Spots and tap Start activation.
2. Under References, choose the programme, type the reference and tap Add: `VK-0921` for POTA, `VK3/VC-012` for SOTA, `VKFF-0921` for WWFF, `OC-001` for IOTA.
3. Add more references for more parks. One activation holds at most 1 summit, 1 WWFF reference and 1 island.
4. Under Station, check Callsign on air and Grid, or tap Use GPS.
5. Tap Start activation.

POTA and SOTA references show suggestions as you type. Find nearby parks and summits lists those near your GPS position and sets the grid unless you typed one.

FT8 and FT4 messages carry the activation's Callsign on air and grid in place of Settings › Station.

## Progress

The strip above the waterfall shows the activation, such as `POTA VK-0921 · 7 of 10`, and the time left in the UTC day. Tap it to open the activation in Spots. With several references, it shows the first with a target and the number of others.

Each programme's target:

- POTA: 10 QSOs in one UTC day. A repeat of the same call, band and mode counts once.
- SOTA: 4 different stations in one UTC day.
- WWFF: 44 QSOs, added up over every activation at the reference. A repeat of the same call, band, mode and UTC day counts once.
- IOTA: no minimum.

A reference that reaches its target reads Qualified. Tap a QSO in the activation to edit it.

## Dupes in an activation

Dupes count only QSOs in the running activation, so a new activation starts with none, even at the same park. [Dupes](making-qsos.md#dupes) has the rules.

## Editing and ending

Edit activation changes the references, callsign, grid and Wavelog station. The QSOs and dupes stay with the activation.

End activation… asks once and names the QSOs it keeps on the device. Tap End activation, or Keep operating to go on.

## Where the QSOs go

Each QSO logged during the activation carries its references.

- Wavelog: the QSOs go to the station location chosen for the activation.
- Files: Settings › Logging turns on a POTA file for pota.app, a SOTA file for the SOTA database, a WWFF file for your WWFF coordinator and an ADIF file with all fields. Files hold only QSOs made in an activation.

After the activation, in Spots, shows what is waiting, such as POTA file ready, and opens Log › Activations. Tap Share to send a file, and Mark submitted once you have uploaded it.

RR73 downloads the park and summit lists for the suggestions while Activations is on, and keeps them on the device.
