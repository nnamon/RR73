# Experiment 0005: RTTY decoder against minimodem

Status: accepted 2026-10-14. Measured on the tree of commit 85f41ab.

## Decision

- RR73 receives RTTY with its own decoder, `rr73-rtty`: mark and space matched filters with automatic threshold correction, the Viterbi framer, and a squelch that judges each character on its own SNR, at -14 dB in 3000 Hz by default.
- On the synthetic sets at 45.45 baud and 170 Hz shift it reaches 10 % CER 1.5 dB below minimodem 0.24 in AWGN (-9.1 dB against -7.6 dB) and 2.1 to 5.3 dB below it with CCIR fading. It prints 0.1 characters per minute of noise; minimodem prints 20. It costs 0.40 ms of CPU per second of audio natively and about 0.6 ms in Chromium and WebKit.
- The app reaches it through `rr73-wasm`: `RttyReceiver` takes 12 kHz chunks and returns characters and events as JSON; `RttyTransmitter` renders keyboard text at the AudioContext rate; `rtty_encode` renders one transmission.
- The Viterbi framer is the default. The UART framer needs 1.9 dB more SNR for 10 % CER in AWGN and does not reach 10 % CER with CCIR poor fading; it stays as an option for its delay of one character, against two.

## Question

How does `rr73-rtty` compare with an established decoder on the same signals: character error rate (CER) against SNR in AWGN and CCIR fading, characters printed from noise, and CPU time per second of audio? And what do the WebAssembly exports cost in a browser?

## Method

- Signals: `rr73-bench rtty synth` (`docs/bench.md`, RTTY). The `rr73-rtty` encoder keys 45.45 baud, 170 Hz shift, 1.5 stop bits, mark 1585 Hz and space 1415 Hz, with continuous phase, 0.25-bit raised-cosine transitions and 3 LTRS before the text. Each file holds 60 s of keying with 1 s of noise before and after. The text mixes callsigns, serial numbers, contest and conversation words, and uses only characters both figures tables print alike.
- Channel: `rr73-chansim` continuous channel with white noise and the SNR in 3000 Hz, plus Watterson fading at the CCIR 520 settings. 3 files per step: AWGN from -16 to 0 dB in 1 dB steps; fading from -12 dB to +12 dB (good) or +16 dB (moderate, poor) in 2 dB steps. 30 noise-only files of 60 s. 210 files, 244 min of audio.
- Reference: minimodem 0.24, the Ubuntu 24.04 package, run as a binary in the `minimodem` container by `tools/minimodem/decode.sh`: `minimodem --rx -q -f <file> -M 1585 -S 1415 rtty`, at its default confidence threshold and with `--confidence 1.0`, its most sensitive setting tried. minimodem's source was not read. It returns to letters after a space, as the encoder assumes: text such as "599 K7FJK" printed correctly.
- `rr73-rtty`: the default `DecoderConfig` (Viterbi framer with a delay of 2 characters, ATC over 4 bits, AFC tracking, squelch -14 dB), and the same without the squelch and with the UART framer. Both decoders get the exact tones and neither searches.
- CER: edit distance between the text sent and the text received over the characters sent, after CR LF becomes LF and control characters are dropped. Characters printed from the noise around a transmission count as insertions. The SNR for 1 % and 10 % CER is interpolated in log CER between the steps either side of the last crossing.
- CPU: one thread for `rr73-rtty` (`--threads 1`, decoding only); one process at a time for minimodem (`RR73_JOBS=1`, user plus system time, which includes reading the file and starting the process). Apple M4, Docker Desktop with 10 CPUs, load average 4 to 5.
- Browser cost: `tools/wasm-cost/rtty.mjs` in the `e2e` container (Playwright 1.63) with the release build of `rr73-wasm`. The receiver gets 78.8 s of keying at -5 dB in 512-sample chunks, the capture worklet's size, and the page parses every event. The transmitter renders 0.1 s blocks at 48 kHz. Median of 7 runs, load average 3 to 4.

### The squelch

The first run found a floor of 1.5 % CER in AWGN at every SNR from -6 to 0 dB with the squelch on. The squelch compared the spectrum's SNR, averaged over 0.7 s, when a character left the framer, so noise just before and after each transmission printed: one character before, 2 to 4 after. Each character is now judged on its own SNR: the mark plus space filter power at its 7 bit ends, less the noise both filters pass, against the noise floor. The first character after squelched ones must also reach the threshold in its start bit, and squelched characters no longer change the case (commit 0a0b210).

| Squelch -14 dB | Spectrum SNR (before) | Character SNR (after) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CER at -5 to 0 dB, AWGN | 1.51 to 1.54 % | 0.00 to 0.09 % |
| SNR for 1 % CER, AWGN | not reached | -6.9 dB |
| SNR for 1 % CER, CCIR good | +9.9 dB | +6.7 dB |
| SNR for 10 % CER, CCIR poor | +1.1 dB | +0.4 dB |
| Characters printed per minute of noise | 0.3 | 0.1 |

With the squelch at -16 dB, 1 min of noise printed 5 characters; -12 dB lost copy at -13 to -11 dB on a 30 s trial set. -14 dB costs no copy against no squelch at any SNR where the CER is under 50 %.

## The WebAssembly API

`crates/rr73-wasm/src/rtty.rs`. Settings are JSON in the form of `rr73_rtty::DecoderConfig` and `TxConfig`; missing fields take their defaults, and `rtty_defaults()` returns both.

- `new RttyReceiver(json)`: throws for malformed JSON or values out of range.
- `push(samples)`: 12 kHz mono audio of any length. Returns a JSON array of the events it completed, oldest first: `char` (`ch`, `sample`, `snr_db`, `quality`, `framing_error`), `case` (`letters` or `figures`), `afc` (`mark_hz`, `space_hz`), `tone_shift` (`shift_hz`) and `signal` (`snr_db`, `squelch_open`, every 0.51 s). `sample` counts input samples since creation or `reset`. The same audio gives the same events whatever the chunk sizes. Characters arrive about 0.33 s after their stop bit, the framer's delay.
- `flush()`, `tune(mark_hz)`, `find(near_hz, range_hz)`, `set_afc_tracking(on)`, `reset()`, `config()`, and the getters `mark_hz`, `space_hz`, `shift_hz`, `snr_db` and `samples`.
- `new RttyTransmitter(json)` with `sample_rate` set to the AudioContext rate; `push_text(text)` returns the number of characters skipped; `render(max_samples)` returns fewer samples only once the transmission has ended; `finish()`; getters `is_done` and `queued_s`. With the queue empty it sends the diddle (LTRS).
- `rtty_encode(text, json)` renders one transmission. `rtty_unsendable(text, "us" | "ita2")` lists the characters the figures table cannot send.

## Open risks

- Not tested on a device. Safari on iOS runs JavaScriptCore on a phone CPU; WebKit 26.6 on Linux in Docker stands in for it.
- Synthetic signals only. Both decoders saw the `rr73-rtty` encoder through `rr73-chansim`. Radios keying FSK or AFSK with other transitions, timing jitter, stop lengths and filters, and real noise, QRM and adjacent RTTY signals, are untested. No KiwiSDR recordings yet.
- The squelch takes its noise floor from the median of the spectrum over 200 to 3000 Hz. In a contest band full of signals the median rises and weak characters may be squelched. Unverified on recordings.
- A LTRS or FIGS received below the squelch is lost, so figures can print as letters until the next shift, or the next space with unshift on space.
- The bench measures 45.45 baud and 170 Hz with the tones given. 50 and 75 baud, other shifts, reverse polarity, the AFC search and drift are covered only by the crate's tests.
- minimodem ran with exact tones at two confidence settings; its other options (bandwidth, auto-carrier) were not tried.
- The fading results rest on 3 files of 60 s per step; above +10 dB the spread between files exceeds the difference between the decoders.
